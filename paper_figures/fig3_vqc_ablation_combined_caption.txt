Figure 3. VQC encoding ablation: amplitude (75 mRMR genes, 7 qubits) vs. angle (10 mRMR genes, 10 qubits), identical nested LOOCV/ansatz/training budget. (A) Mean per-fold training accuracy vs. LOOCV test accuracy -- VQC-amplitude gap -0.043 (0.671 vs 0.714), VQC-angle gap +0.305 (0.638 vs 0.333), consistent with overfitting on the smaller feature set. (B) Final LOOCV accuracy for each encoding, with exact McNemar test on paired per-fold correctness: p=0.0215. Note this ablation confounds encoding scheme with feature-set size; see Discussion/Limitations for interpretation.
